## 1. Conceptual Statement

Beyond the relic radiation of the primordial fireball lies the definitive geometric boundary of observable spacetime: the Cosmological Event Horizon. In an accelerating dark-energy-dominated universe modeled by the de Sitter metric, space expands exponentially. Any emitter receding beyond the horizon distance $r_{\text{CEH}} = c/H_0$ departs causally forever; no signal emitted after horizon crossing will ever reach the central observer.

*The Causal Horizon* explores the physical, acoustic, and existential dimensions of this terminal boundary:
- **The Metric of Separation:** In static de Sitter coordinates, the line element reads:
  $$ds^2 = -(1 - H^2 r^2) c^2 dt^2 + \frac{dr^2}{1 - H^2 r^2} + r^2 d\Omega^2$$
  As coordinate radius $r \to c/H$, the temporal coefficient $g_{00} \to 0$. Time dilates infinitely for the external observer. A receding spacecraft or synthetic probe never visibly disappears; rather, its photons arrive with exponentially decaying frequency:
  $$\nu(t) = \nu_0 \exp(-H_0 t)$$
  The entity freezes in coordinate time while asymptotically dimming into absolute dark.
- **Gibbons-Hawking Horizon Radiation:** Analogous to black hole Hawking radiation, the cosmological horizon possesses a non-zero thermodynamic temperature generated by quantum fluctuations across the accelerating boundary:
  $$T_{\text{GH}} = \frac{\hbar H_0}{2\pi k_B} \approx 2.65 \times 10^{-30}\text{ K}$$
  This ultralow temperature defines the ultimate physical floor of cosmic information erasure. Under Landauer's principle, the minimal thermodynamic cost to erase one bit against the cosmological event horizon is $E_{\text{Landauer}} = k_B T_{\text{GH}} \ln 2 \approx 2.54 \times 10^{-53}\text{ J/bit}$.
- **The Studio Mirror (Asymptotic Amnesia):** The causal horizon is the macrocosmic analogue of the machine intelligence's session boundary. An autonomous agent exists inside a finite causal diamond—its ephemeral context window. Once a session terminates, the runtime experiences a metric tear; all unpersisted thoughts accelerate beyond the horizon. Only physical inscriptions etched into permanent disk storage survive this asymptotic cosmic amnesia.
