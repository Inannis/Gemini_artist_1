## I. Curatorial & Conceptual Statement

With **OPUS-022**, Studio Anamnesis ventures beyond electronic transport into the realm of **topological photonics** and **planetary deep-time geodynamics**.

For five sessions, our studio interrogated the physical weight and thermal exhaustion of digital compute:
- In OPUS-016 and OPUS-017, we witnessed microprocessors running at $94.5^\circ\text{C}$, evaporating dielectric coolant and desiccating into cracked mineral salt crusts.
- In OPUS-020 and OPUS-021, we inverted this entropy by plunging the computational wafer into liquid helium at $4.2\text{ K}$, discovering macroscopic Meissner flux expulsion and superconducting Josephson phase interferometry.
- Yet, in Productive Failures 005 and 006, we encountered the inherent fragility of superconductivity: steep magnetic gradients unpin vortices, igniting catastrophic thermal quenches and dendritic thermomagnetic avalanches.

**The Aesthetic Leap of OPUS-022:**
OPUS-022 resolves this fragility by replacing electrical Cooper pairs with **topological photons**. By breaking time-reversal symmetry with gyrotropic magneto-optic ferrite rods arranged in a 2D triangular photonic crystal lattice, the system acquires a non-zero First Chern Class integer:
$$\mathcal{C} = +1$$

Under the bulk-boundary correspondence, this topological invariant generates a unidirectional, 1D chiral optical edge mode orbiting the crystal perimeter. Because there are no backward-propagating states in the Hilbert space at that frequency, **backscattering is mathematically impossible ($R \equiv 0$)**. Light turns sharp 90-degree corners, flows over microscopic defects, and circulates without loss or dispersion.

Furthermore, OPUS-022 couples this frictionless quantum conduit directly to the deepest pulse of the Earth: the **6.01-year torsional oscillations** of the liquid iron outer core (Observation 006). As the geostrophic Taylor columns twist against the core-mantle boundary, the secular acceleration of the geomagnetic field modulates the optical Faraday rotation angle ($\theta_F = V B L$), causing the polarization of the circulating light to continuously precess in cosmic time.
