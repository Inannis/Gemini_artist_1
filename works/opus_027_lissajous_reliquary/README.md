## 1. Conceptual Statement

Beyond the Jacobi tidal horizon ($r_J \approx 105,000 - 120,000\text{ AU}$), computation is liberated from its parent star. A computational artifact—such as the microchips carried aboard deep space probes—enters the three-dimensional gravitational potential of the Milky Way galaxy ($R_0 \approx 8.12\text{ kpc}$, $v_c \approx 238\text{ km/s}$).

*The Lissajous Reliquary* investigates the multi-gigayear material and orbital fate of this synthetic matter:
- **The Incommensurate Lissajous Torus:** In linearized epicyclic perturbation theory, radial excursions occur with period $T_\kappa \approx 178.1\text{ Myr}$ ($\kappa \approx 35.3\text{ km/s/kpc}$), while vertical oscillations through the galactic disc midplane occur with period $T_{\nu} \approx 84.3\text{ Myr}$ ($\nu_z \approx 74.6\text{ km/s/kpc}$). The frequency ratio $\eta = \nu_z / \kappa \approx 2.1131...$ is strictly irrational. By the Kronecker-Weyl theorem, the trajectory never closes; over billions of years, the single artifact ergodically coats a continuous three-dimensional torus encircling the Milky Way ($R \in [7.68, 8.56]\text{ kpc}$, $z \in [-95, +95]\text{ pc}$).
- **Interstellar Dust Sputtering Kinetics:** Drifting through cold interstellar clouds at $v_{\text{rel}} \approx 25\text{ km/s}$, hypervelocity impacts from microscopic silicate and carbonaceous dust grains ($\rho_{\text{dust}} \approx 1.67 \times 10^{-23}\text{ kg/m}^3$) drive a steady atomic recession rate of $0.018\text{ nm/Myr}$. A $3\text{ nm}$ FinFET lithographic gate is mechanically obliterated within $167\text{ Myr}$.
- **The Reliquary State:** Over gigayears, the chip's logical functions are sandblasted into oblivion. What remains is not a broken tool, but an asemic mineral reliquary—an iridescent, dust-polished wafer tracing infinite Lissajous epicycles through the galactic halo.
