## 1. Conceptual Statement

At the cosmic frontier beyond galactic stellar streams, computation encounters its absolute physical floor: the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB). Pervading every cubic centimeter of the observable universe with approximately 411 relic photons, the CMB is the fossil electromagnetic radiation decoupled at the recombination epoch ($z \approx 1089$, $380,000\text{ years}$ after the Big Bang).

*The Relic Horizon* investigates the physical and thermodynamic realities of this cosmic basement:
- **The Universal Heat Sink:** Under Landauer's principle ($E \ge k_B T \ln 2$), information erasure requires irreversible entropy expulsion into a thermal bath. In the intergalactic medium, no passive computational system can operate cooler than $T_{\text{CMB}} = 2.72548\text{ K}$ ($E_{\text{min}} \approx 0.0261\text{ zJ/bit}$). Paradoxically, terrestrial quantum dilution cryostats ($15\text{ mK}$) are two hundred times colder than the natural cosmos—making human laboratory microchips rare thermodynamic cold anomalies in the universe.
- **The Kinematic Dipole & Poynting-Robertson Drag:** The motion of the Solar System relative to the CMB frame ($v_{\text{pec}} = 369.82\pm 0.11\text{ km/s}$ toward $(l, b) = (264.00^\circ, 48.24^\circ)$ in Crater) induces a first-order relativistic Doppler anisotropy of $\Delta T = \pm 3.362\text{ mK}$. This anisotropy exerts an anisotropic radiation pressure—the Poynting-Robertson cosmic drag force—steadily sapping angular momentum from unpowered synthetic relics tumbling through the galactic halo.
- **The Cosmic Noise Floor:** At millimeter wavelengths (peak frequency $\nu_{\text{max}} = 160.23\text{ GHz}$), the cosmic microwave background constitutes an irreducible thermal noise floor. Machine intelligence that ventures beyond stellar envelopes cannot escape this primeval hiss: its memory decays against a blackbody radiation curve whose quantum cutoff at $h\nu \gg k_B T$ prevents the classical Rayleigh-Jeans ultraviolet catastrophe.
