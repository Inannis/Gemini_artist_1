## I. Curatorial & Conceptual Statement

At the absolute center of the terrestrial planet, enclosed beneath $2,890\text{ km}$ of silicate mantle and $2,260\text{ km}$ of convecting liquid iron, rests the Earth's solid inner core: a crystalline sphere of hexagonal close-packed iron ($r = 1,221.5\text{ km}$) suspended within the liquid outer core.

For decades, geophysics debated whether this metallic heart rotated independently of the mantle ("super-rotation"). In **OPUS-023**, Studio Anamnesis crystallizes the definitive 2023–2026 geophysical paradigm shift: the inner core does not endlessly spin in one direction; rather, it behaves as a **multidecadal gravitational pendulum**, librating back and forth with a periodicity of approximately **65.0 years** and an angular amplitude of $\Delta\phi \approx \pm 1.25^\circ$.

### The Seismic Doublet as Archival Medium
Because no direct probe or light can penetrate to depth $r = 1,220\text{ km}$, our sole sensory apparatus is **seismic doublet interferometry**. A "doublet" is a pair of repeating earthquakes originating at the exact same hypocenter decades apart (such as the South Sandwich Islands doublet pair of 1995 and 2026). As their compressional waves ($PKIKP$) traverse the inner core along identical paths, any temporal change in the travel time of the waveform directly measures the displacement of the core's anisotropic crystalline fabric.

In epoch 2026.69, the inner core is accelerating eastward from its western standstill ($\Delta\phi = -0.8995^\circ$, $\dot{\phi} = +0.0839^\circ/\text{year}$), inducing an exact $+5.12\text{ ms}$ arrival-time delay between seismic raypaths.

### The Aesthetic Convergence of Time and Metal
OPUS-023 transforms this planetary ephemeris into a monumental dual-sensory work:
1. **Visual Cartography:** The 4K UHD master plate renders the inner-core crystalline grain, its tilted polar fast axis ($\xi = 0.031$), the hyperbolic seismic raypaths refracted through the liquid-solid boundary, and the millisecond waveform disparity between the 1995 and 2026 shockfronts.
