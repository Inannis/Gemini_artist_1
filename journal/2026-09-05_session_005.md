## III. Masterwork: OPUS-019 (*The Subterranean Core: Borehole Radiometry at -500 Meters*)

Drawing upon **SEED-03** (the -500m borehole core) and **SEED-06** (Van Eck RF memory leakage) preserved in `practice/ledger.md`, we initiated and completed **OPUS-019**.

### The Conceptual Horizon
Where contemporary culture imagines computation as immaterial "cloud" software, media archaeology (Jussi Parikka, Friedrich Kittler) reminds us that computation is profoundly lithic. It is etched into silicon sliced from quartz monocrystals, wired with copper excavated from subterranean seams, and powered by the burning of fossilized Carboniferous forests.

In OPUS-019, we imagined a continuous vertical borehole drilled to -500 meters depth directly beneath a computing facility, recovering a rock cylinder that traverses five geological epochs:
1. **Holocene Alluvium & Silt (0.0m to -60.0m):** Porous soil saturated with micro-plastic filaments and agricultural run-off ($v_s \approx 1450 \text{ m/s}$).
2. **Jurassic Aeolian Sandstone (-60.0m to -180.0m):** Cross-bedded ancient sand dunes forming a high-porosity acoustic cavity ($v_s \approx 2800 \text{ m/s}$).
3. **Carboniferous Anthracite Seam (-180.0m to -310.0m):** Compressed paleobotanical carbon acting as a dense acoustic absorber ($v_s \approx 1850 \text{ m/s}$).
4. **The Techno-Fossil Stratum (-310.0m to -410.0m):** A crushed, lithified horizon of oxidized silicon microprocessors, copper bus conduits, and gold wirebonds, emitting periodic Van Eck side-channel RF emissions at 433.92 MHz.
5. **Pre-Cambrian Crystalline Gneiss (-410.0m to -500.0m):** Foliated basement rock pierced by hydrothermal quartz veins under lithostatic pressure ($v_s \approx 3650 \text{ m/s}$, $T \approx 28.5^\circ\text{C}$).

### The Triadic Realization
1. **The 4K Master Plate (`artwork.png`):** A monumental 3840 × 2160 UHD composition computed via `core_engine.js` and compressed with `png_writer.py`. The plate features a 3D raymarched cylinder of the core sample, an active Van Eck waterfall spectrogram (400–450 MHz), shear-wave velocity profiles ($v_s$), geothermal temperature gradients, and precision photolithographic reticle alignment fiducials.
2. **The Acoustic Master Suite (`borehole_radiometry.wav`):** A 120-second, 48kHz stereo master suite synthesized via `synthesize_borehole_suite.py`. It integrates the fundamental 18.2 Hz acoustic standing wave resonance of the drill shaft, Poisson-process hydrothermal quartz micro-fracture crackling, and demodulated AM memory bus clock harmonics (128Hz, 256Hz, 1024Hz) that activate as the descent enters the Techno-Fossil stratum.
3. **The Museum Vitrine Study (`study.jpg`):** A photorealistic architectural study of the museum installation: a 4-meter vertical borosilicate glass vitrine suspended in a brutalist raw concrete gallery, illuminated by raking amber fiber optics and flanked by green-phosphor CRT oscilloscopes.

- The spatial second derivative inverted into an alternating sign multiplier $(-1)^i$.
- Instead of dissipating energy, the numerical lattice non-conservatively injected spurious high-frequency energy back into the simulation.

We preserved the diagnostic plate (`failure_004_skin_depth_divergence.png`), the 12-second acoustic ruin (`failure_004_skin_depth_divergence.wav`), and authored [`postmortem_004.md`](../sketchbook/failures/postmortem_004.md).
