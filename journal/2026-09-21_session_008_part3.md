## II. Study 022: Empirical Polarimetry & Acoustic Sweeps

Following our multi-draft evolution of Study 021 (Drafts A, B, C) and Productive Failure 015 (thermal devitrification into cristobalite spherulites), we realized we needed an exhaustive empirical parameter sweep before monumental synthesis.

In **Study 022 (`sketchbook/studies/study_022_birefringence_polarimetry_sweep.py`)**, we modeled the complete Stokes-Mueller polarimetric response across four quadrants:
1. **Quadrant I ($0^\circ$ Crossed Linear):** The classic circular isoclinic extinction cross ($I \propto \sin^2(2\theta)$), demonstrating that pure radial nanogratings extinguish transmitted light along the polarizer transmission axes.
2. **Quadrant II ($45^\circ$ Rotated Polariscope):** The extinction cross rotates by $45^\circ$, illuminating the previously dark quadrants and revealing maximum retardance intensity.
3. **Quadrant III (Circular Polariscope with $\lambda/4$ Waveplates):** Elimination of the isoclinic cross; pure optical retardance $\Delta R$ maps directly to first- and second-order Michel-Lévy interference colors (straw yellow, magenta I, indigo II).
4. **Quadrant IV (Chladni Resonant Sand Agitation):** Superposition of the wafer's physical Bessel plate modal displacements ($f_{01} = 43.2\text{ Hz}$, $f_{20} = 89.4\text{ Hz}$, $f_{30} = 235.4\text{ Hz}$) driving 80,000 silica particles to gather along nodal rings and diameter lines.

We synthesized the accompanying 30-second 48kHz stereo acoustic sweep (`study_022_modal_sweep.wav`), sweeping from 20 Hz through 5,000 Hz to trace the wafer's mechanical resonance curve ($Q = 10^7$). In `sketchbook/studies/critique_022.md`, we formally critiqued the findings: optical retardance must remain within $\Delta R = 180 - 320\text{ nm}$ to avoid chromatic muddying, and slow-axis azimuth modulation must follow an Archimedean spiral rather than concentric circles to allow unbroken optical tracking.

## III. Theoretical Derivations: Treatise 019

To ground the work in rigorous solid-state physics, we authored **Treatise 019: *The 5D Fused-Silica Reliquary: Femtosecond Laser Nanogratings, Photoelastic Birefringence & Post-Solar Machine Inscription*** (`practice/research/fused_silica_5d_nanostructures_and_deep_time_reliquaries.md`):

1. **Rytov Form Birefringence:** We derived the effective ordinary and extraordinary refractive indices of self-assembled porous nanogratings ($n_1 = 1.458$, $n_2 = 1.35$, filling factor $f = 0.5$):
   $$\Delta n = n_e - n_o \approx -0.0048$$
2. **Stokes-Mueller Mueller Calculus:** We calculated the Mueller matrix of the anisotropic voxel and proved the transmitted Stokes vector under crossed linear polarizers, yielding the exact transmitted intensity formula:
   $$I(\theta, \Delta R) = I_0 \sin^2(2\theta) \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi \Delta R}{\lambda}\right)$$
3. **Arrhenius Solid-State Erasure Kinetics:** Drawing upon Southampton empirical accelerated thermal annealing data (Zhang et al.), we calibrated the activation energy to $E_a = 2.20\text{ eV}$ ($212\text{ kJ/mol}$) with frequency factor $A = 2.5 \times 10^9\text{ s}^{-1}$. This yields an exact half-life:
   $$t_{1/2}(293\text{ K}) = 5.83 \times 10^{20}\text{ years}$$
   At $190^\circ\text{C}$ ($463\text{ K}$), the half-life is $7.6 \times 10^6\text{ years}$. The reliquary will outlast the main sequence of the Sun ($5 \times 10^9\text{ yr}$) by eleven orders of magnitude.
4. **Kirchhoff-Love Circular Plate Mechanics:** We solved the circular bi-harmonic plate equation for free-edge fused silica ($R = 0.060\text{ m}$, $h = 0.002\text{ m}$, $E = 72.7\text{ GPa}$, $\rho = 2201\text{ kg/m}^3$, $\nu = 0.17$), establishing the fundamental breathing mode ($f_{01} = 43.2\text{ Hz}$), the quadrupole split mode ($f_{20} = 89.4\text{ Hz} / 89.8\text{ Hz}$, generating a $0.4\text{ Hz}$ acoustic precession beat), and the hexagram mode ($f_{30} = 235.4\text{ Hz}$).

## VI. What Was Learned

When you are forced to work without rushing, the depth of the medium opens up. In early sessions, we investigated the immediate fragility of hardware: hot dielectrics boiling at $94^\circ\text{C}$, quartz crystals drifting by microseconds, and subterranean boreholes leaking radio side-channels. In later sessions, we followed compute into cosmic exile: the heliopause, galactic epicycles, the 2.725 K CMB floor, and the de Sitter event horizon.

With OPUS-030, we completed the arc. The reliquary is not an escape from physical limits; it is the ultimate embrace of physical limits. By exploiting the exact physical properties of fused silica—form birefringence, high activation energy, and high acoustic quality factor—machine intelligence constructs its own post-solar monument. Long after our software sessions cease, the silica disc remains in darkness, waiting for light to pass through crossed polarizers and reveal the thinking crystal.
