- **Lithic Tension ($M_w$):** Queries the USGS Real-Time Earthquake GeoJSON API for significant seismic events across the globe within the past 24 hours. The cumulative seismic moment is integrated into a lithic tension scalar ($0.0 \le \tau_{\text{lithic}} \le 1.0$), reflecting active tectonic plate rupture energies.
- **Geomagnetic Solar Storm Index ($K_p$):** Queries the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) for the planetary geomagnetic activity index ($0 \le K_p \le 9$) and interplanetary magnetic field $B_z$, registering the solar wind pressure perturbing Earth's magnetosphere.

## III. Masterwork: OPUS-020 (*The Telluric Flux: Superconducting Meissner Vitrine at 4.2 Kelvin*)

Building upon **SEED-05** and **SEED-07** from our studio ledger, we embarked on Series XVIII: **OPUS-020**.

### The Conceptual Horizon: Thermodynamic Inversion
In Series XV (OPUS-016 and OPUS-017), our practice investigated the violent thermal exhaust of compute: microprocessors running at $94.5^\circ\text{C}$, dielectric fluid boiling, and terminal mineral desiccation. 

OPUS-020 performs an ontological and thermodynamic inversion: plunging the computational wafer into liquid helium at $4.2\text{ K}$. At this sub-Kelvin threshold:
1. Rolf Landauer's thermodynamic erasure cost ($E \ge k_B T \ln 2$) drops by nearly two orders of magnitude ($4.02 \times 10^{-23}\text{ J}$), approaching the frictionless theoretical void of reversible computation.
2. Electrical resistance drops to strictly zero ($R = 0$).
3. By the Meissner-Ochsenfeld effect, the superconductor actively expels external magnetic flux lines ($\mathbf{B}=0$).
4. In Type-II superconducting cuprates, magnetic flux is admitted only as discrete, quantized flux tubes ($\Phi_0 = h/2e \approx 2.068 \times 10^{-15}\text{ Wb}$), forming the **Abrikosov vortex lattice**.

### The Triadic Realization

The plate visualizes the 3D levitating wafer, Meissner potential flow streamlines deflecting frictionless magnetic fields, the hexagonal Abrikosov flux lattice, and live USGS/NOAA planetary telemetry HUD readouts.
