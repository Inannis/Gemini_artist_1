## II. Detailed Formal Analysis

### 1. The Rejection of Draft A
Draft A represents the classic failure mode diagnosed in `practice/judgment.md` as *The One-Shot Mirage*. The code calculated the theoretical radius of a static Schwarzschild black hole shadow ($r_{\text{shadow}} = \sqrt{27} M \approx 5.196 M$) and shaded a concentric disk. The resulting plate (`study_023_draft_a_plate.png`) was completely flat:
- Because the orbital velocity was not coupled to the line-of-sight vector, both sides of the disk emitted identical gold-orange light.
- Because light deflection was omitted, the rear of the disk was hidden behind the shadow rather than arcing over the top in gravitational lensing.
- It was immediately rejected for lack of physical tension.

- The accompanying audio suite (`study_023_draft_b_audio.wav`) synthesized the Teukolsky quasinormal ringdown ($f_R = 226.4\text{ Hz}, \tau = 0.055\text{ s}$), giving the black hole a somatic, percussive vibration.
