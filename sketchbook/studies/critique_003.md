## 1. Description of the Evidence

A 2400 × 1600 px plate showing 84 procedural glyphs arranged in a 12 × 7 horizontal register on a dark slate tablet. Each glyph is assembled stochastically from 3 to 7 primitive elements: cuneiform triangular wedges, circular arcs, Cartesian coordinate lines, concentric rings, and coordinate crosses. Palette: titanium white, pale gold, and faint cyan register lines.

---

## 2. Evaluation Against Studio Criteria (`practice/judgment.md`)

### A. Material/Algorithmic Resistance: **Weak (4/10)**
The script runs in 0.24s and outputs vector shapes directly onto a flat color fill. There is no physical modeling of stone, no chisel gouging, no depth shadow, no ink absorption, and no geological fracture. It feels like an illustration of an idea rather than an encounter with a resistant material.

### B. Structural Cohesion of Glyphs: **Inconsistent (5/10)**
Some glyphs achieve genuine elegance (notably glyphs with interlocking wedges and concentric rings that resemble ancient astronomical notations or Babylonian astrological symbols). However, many glyphs feel fragmented—isolated floating crosses and detached arcs that lack internal morphological tension or ligature. In true written language, strokes hold one another; they participate in a shared gestural center of gravity.

### C. Aesthetic Necessity & Ambiguity: **Promising (7/10)**
The fundamental concept—synthetic glyphs that refuse semantic translation—is potent. The viewer's brain attempts to read them as Sumerian, Greek, musical notation, or circuit logic, only to slip into semantic suspension. This tension is worth pursuing deeply.

## 3. Prescriptions for Elevation to Opus (OPUS-015)

To elevate this inquiry into **OPUS-015: The Autoregressive Ghost (Asemic Inscriptions on Crystalline Slate)**, we must:

1. **Procedural Chisel Depth & Mineral Substrate:**
   Implement a 3D heightfield/bump-map renderer in Python/NumPy simulating dark volcanic basalt slate. The glyph strokes must cut deep V-shaped grooves into the stone, casting directional micro-shadows under oblique raking gallery light.
2. **Gold Leaf Filling & Craquelure:**
   The carved grooves must be selectively illuminated with gold leaf that has cracked and flaked along geological fault lines.
3. **Compound Morphological Glyphs:**
   Re-engineer the glyph grammar: each glyph must be built around a central vertebral stem with subordinate radical limbs, resembling a cross between Sumerian cuneiform, Tibetan Uchen script, and high-energy Feynman particle collision diagrams.
4. **Scale:**
   Upgrade from 2400×1600 to full 3840×2160 (4K UHD) Master Plate.
