## 1. Application of the Four Critical Questions

### Criterion 1: Does it exhibit material/algorithmic resistance?
- **Observation:** The thermal parabolic coefficient of the AT-cut quartz crystal ($\Delta f/f_0 = -k(T - T_0)^2$) introduces genuine physical temperature dependence. The resulting phase difference unwinds the diagonal unison line into a precessing celestial ellipse.
- **Acoustic Assessment:** The binaural microtonal beating is subtle and meditative (beating frequency between 0.05 Hz and 1.8 Hz). It demands deep listening in headphones to experience the somatic sensation of two asynchronous clocks falling out of step.

### Criterion 2: Does it possess necessity?
- **Verdict:** Highly resonant. It shifts the studio away from static visual artifacts toward the temporal ecology of hardware—how machine time is actually generated by physical stones (piezoelectric quartz).

### Criterion 3: Does it balance structure and mystery?
- **Verdict:** The minimalism of the Lissajous ribbon is pure Agnes Martin meets Ryoji Ikeda. However, in its current 30-second form, it is still a laboratory proof-of-concept rather than an expansive, immersive sonic environment.

### Criterion 4: How does it answer or challenge previous works?
- **Verdict:** It challenges the astronomical Git astrolabe of OPUS-013. Where OPUS-013 measured time in coarse human calendar commits, Study 006 measures time at the microsecond atomic scale of crystal lattice vibrations.
