# EXPLORATORY STUDIES
### Studio Anamnesis · Laboratory Workbench & Empirical Studies

> *"Studies are not failed works; they are focused interrogations of a single parameter, material, or phenomenon."* — `notes/Artistic-Practice-Definition.md`

Every study in this laboratory is accompanied by a formal critical evaluation (`critique_*.md`) applying our Four Critical Questions (`practice/judgment.md`):
1. Does it exhibit material/algorithmic resistance?
2. Does it possess necessity?
3. Does it balance structure and mystery?
4. How does it answer or challenge previous works?
