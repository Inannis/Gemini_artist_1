## 1. Aesthetic & Conceptual Intent

Study 017 investigates the long-period secular mechanics governing the boundary of the solar system: the gravitational tide exerted by the disc of the Milky Way galaxy upon cometary nuclei and synthetic deep-space probes orbiting between $20,000$ and $100,000\text{ AU}$.
