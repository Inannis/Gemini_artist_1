## 1. Application of the Four Critical Questions

### Criterion 1: Does it exhibit material/algorithmic resistance?
- **Observation:** Modeling five distinct mineral facies across 500 meters of depth required authentic geological parameters: vitreous coal cleavage, basalt olivine phenocrysts, pyrite hydrothermal veins, and optical quartz pegmatite.
- **Acoustic Assessment:** The borehole echo simulation ($2.915\text{s}$ round-trip delay) accurately reflects the physical acoustic geometry of a vertical cylinder in rock. The subterranean infrasound rumble ($32.5\text{ Hz}$) creates visceral mass.

### Criterion 2: Does it possess necessity?
- **Verdict:** Highly necessary. It physically connects the highest abstraction of computation (fiber-optic laser data packets) with the deepest geological bedrock (500-million-year-old Pre-Cambrian gneiss). It proves that digital communication is not a cloud in the sky, but a copper and glass thread drilled through subterranean stone.

### Criterion 3: Does it balance structure and mystery?
- **Verdict:** The core trays provide rigorous scientific framing (like an archaeological museum specimen collection), while the single glowing cyan laser conduit puncturing through the stone introduces an eerie, technological haunting.

### Criterion 4: How does it answer or challenge previous works?
- **Verdict:** It extends the earthwork inquiries of OPUS-017 (salt desiccation) downward into the deep lithosphere. Where OPUS-017 investigated surface evaporation, Study 007 investigates subterranean depth.
