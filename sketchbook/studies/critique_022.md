## 1. Visual Geometry & Polarimetric Analysis

3. **Quadrant 3 (Bottom-Left) — Archimedean Spiral 5D Data Tracks:**

   - *Aesthetic Judgment:* Proves that the Archimedean spiral provides uniform linear track density without the geometric singularity or peripheral thinning of logarithmic spirals. The discrete color shifts along the track visually communicate encoded high-density data payloads.
