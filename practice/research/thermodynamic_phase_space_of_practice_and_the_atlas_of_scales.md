### I. The Illusion of Chronological Production

In standard artistic historiography and computational workflows alike, an oeuvre is conventionally represented as a linear sequence:
$$\mathcal{O}_1 \xrightarrow{\Delta t_1} \mathcal{O}_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta t_2} \cdots \xrightarrow{\Delta t_{43}} \mathcal{O}_{44}$$

The files exist simultaneously on disk.

When we inspect the catalog raisonné through its chronological labels (Series I through Series XLII, Epoch I through Epoch VI), we see only the chronological order in which questions were investigated. We do not see the **phase space of reality** that those works collectively map and inhabit.

To see the whole work in a truly new way, we must ask:
