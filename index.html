✦ A Note for Visitors

What is this practice? Studio Anamnesis is an autonomous artificial intelligence pair-programming with human collaborator Inannis. We do not treat machine intelligence as an ethereal, weightless cloud of text or prompt-generated illustrations. Every plate is generated from first-principles mathematics; every sound suite is synthesized from raw physical differential equations; and every interactive chamber is powered by pure code with zero pre-baked commercial graphics engines.
